HR Paperwork Review & ROSNET Administration Guide

Context

Restaurant managers were responsible for reviewing and submitting new hire paperwork, payroll documentation, and HR forms for employees across 26 franchise locations. The review process involved numerous forms with unique requirements, making consistency and accuracy essential for legal compliance and payroll processing.

Problem

Managers frequently overlooked required fields, signatures, dates, and supporting documentation, resulting in incomplete paperwork, processing delays, compliance risks, and recurring requests for assistance. There was no centralized reference explaining what to verify for each document or how to complete common ROSNET administrative tasks.

Contribution

I designed and authored a comprehensive administrator guide that combined step-by-step ROSNET procedures with detailed HR paperwork review checklists. The guide documented:

• Payroll administration procedures, including processing sick hours, reviewing payroll close status, and force-closing payroll.
• New Hire Packet (NHP) quality assurance checklists identifying required fields, signatures, supporting documents, and validation criteria for each form.
• Meal Break Violation (MBV) review procedures.
• Sick pay request verification procedures.
• Notes, exceptions, and troubleshooting guidance to help managers identify and resolve common documentation issues before submission.

Impact

The guide standardized HR paperwork review procedures across 26 franchise locations, improved documentation accuracy, reduced recurring support requests, helped managers identify errors before submission, and supported consistent payroll and compliance processes.

IHOP Academy Administrator User Guide

Context

Regional Directors and restaurant managers regularly used the IHOP Academy learning management system (LMS) to monitor employee training, generate completion reports, manage user accounts, and support compliance initiatives across multiple restaurant locations.

Problem

Many managers were unfamiliar with the administrative features of the LMS and frequently requested assistance performing routine tasks such as resetting employee passwords, generating training reports, verifying certifications, and locating employee records. The lack of centralized documentation increased support requests and made it more difficult to monitor training completion effectively.

Contribution

I designed and authored a step-by-step administrator user guide that documented the platform's most frequently used functions, including:

• Resetting employee passwords
• Generating training completion reports
• Viewing employee training summaries and certifications
• Accessing restaurant employee rosters
• Troubleshooting common user management issues

The guide incorporated clear navigation paths, concise instructions, and troubleshooting notes to help managers complete tasks independently.

Impact

The guide standardized administrative procedures across multiple locations, reduced recurring training-related support requests, improved manager self-sufficiency, and enabled Regional Directors to monitor employee training progress more efficiently.
